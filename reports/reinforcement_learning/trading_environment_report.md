## 1. Executive Summary

This report documents the design, mathematical formulation, numerical unit testing, and empirical baseline benchmarking of `MarketTradingEnv`, the custom Gymnasium trading environment developed for NeuroTrade.

The environment couples a **49-dimensional hybrid observation vector**—incorporating a 32-dimensional latent representation extracted from a frozen 2-layer LSTM market encoder, 14 explicit normalized technical indicators, and a 3-dimensional internal portfolio state—with a discrete action space {`HOLD`, `BUY`, `SELL`}. It enforces realistic execution constraints: strictly long-only trading, zero leverage, cash availability checks, single-trade execution at the subsequent day's Open, mark-to-market portfolio accounting at the Close, realistic transaction fees (0.1% per trade), and an exact logarithmic return reward signal.

### Summary of Key Audit Results:
1. **Gymnasium Compliance**: Passed the official `gymnasium.utils.env_checker.check_env` suite with zero structural or contract violations.
2. **Observation Dimension & Finiteness**: Output vectors strictly adhere to `(49,)` float32 with zero NaNs and zero Infs across all 5,780 chronological samples across train, validation, and test splits.
3. **Execution Timing & Zero Lookahead**: Action selected at day $t$ close $\to$ trade executed at day $t+1$ Open $\to$ portfolio marked at day $t+1$ Close. Future Open and Close prices are strictly masked from the current state observation.
4. **Position Accounting & Churn Mitigation**: Repeated BUY actions while holding a full position and SELL actions while flat are cleanly rejected with ₹0 transaction cost, preventing fee erosion.
5. **Baseline Benchmarks**: Benchmarked against Always HOLD, Buy-and-Hold, and Moving Average Crossover (SMA-10 vs. SMA-50) across all 3 partitions and 5 equity tickers (`RELIANCE`, `TCS`, `INFY`, `HDFCBANK`, `ITC`).

## 2. Environment Architecture & State Representation

### A. The 49-Dimensional Hybrid Observation Space
The state vector $s_t \in \mathbb{R}^{49}$ is partitioned into three distinct sub-spaces:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   HYBRID MARKET OBSERVATION (49-D)                     │
├────────────────────────┬────────────────────────┬──────────────────────┤
│  LSTM Embedding (32-D) │ Technical Features(14) │ Portfolio State (3)  │
│      Indices 0 - 31    │     Indices 32 - 45    │   Indices 46 - 48    │
└────────────────────────┴────────────────────────┴──────────────────────┘
```

1. **Latent Market State Representation ($e_t \in \mathbb{R}^{32}$, Indices $0 \dots 31$)**:
   - Generated by passing the pre-trained, frozen `MarketStateLSTMEncoder` (`improved_rnn_encoder.pt`) over the rolling 30-day sequence of normalized indicators $X_{t-29:t}$.
   - Captures non-linear temporal dynamics, volatility clustering, and trend momentum accumulated across the prior 30 trading sessions.
   - Verified exact numerical identity with pre-computed embeddings saved in `backend/embeddings/`.
2. **Explicit Technical Indicators ($x_t \in \mathbb{R}^{14}$, Indices $32 \dots 45$)**:
   - The instantaneous values of the 14 engineered technical indicators at day $t$ close:
     1. `Daily_Return`
     2. `Log_Return`
     3. `HL_Pct` (Intraday volatility ratio: $(H-L)/C$)
     4. `Rolling_Vol_20` (20-day annualized realized volatility)
     5. `SMA_Ratio_10` ($(C - \text{SMA}_{10})/\text{SMA}_{10}$)
     6. `SMA_Ratio_20`
     7. `SMA_Ratio_50`
     8. `EMA_Ratio_12`
     9. `EMA_Ratio_26`
     10. `RSI_14_Norm` ($\text{RSI}/100 \in [0, 1]$)
     11. `MACD_Line_Norm`
     12. `MACD_Signal_Norm`
     13. `MACD_Hist_Norm`
     14. `Volume_Ratio_20` ($V / \text{SMA}_{20}(V)$)
   - Scaled using the training `StandardScaler` (`data/training/scalers/`) without any lookahead or data snooping.
3. **Internal Portfolio State ($p_t \in \mathbb{R}^3$, Indices $46 \dots 48$)**:
   - Index 46: **Equity Position Fraction** $= \frac{\text{shares}_t \cdot \text{Close}_t}{V_t} \in [0.0, 1.0]$.
   - Index 47: **Available Cash Ratio** $= \frac{\text{cash}_t}{V_t} \in [0.0, 1.0]$.
   - Index 48: **Normalized Portfolio Value** $= \frac{V_t}{\text{initial\_capital}}$.
   - These components inform the RL agent of its immediate liquidity, current equity exposure, and cumulative trajectory relative to the initial capital.

## 3. Action Space, Position-Sizing Rule & Constraints

### Action Space
Discrete action space $\mathcal{A} \in \{0, 1, 2\}$:
- **`Action 0 (HOLD)`**: Maintain existing position. 0 shares bought or sold; 0 transaction costs incurred.
- **`Action 1 (BUY)`**: Allocate available liquid cash to acquire shares at $\text{Open}_{t+1}$.
- **`Action 2 (SELL)`**: Liquidate entire equity position at $\text{Open}_{t+1}$.

### Position-Sizing & Risk Rules
1. **Long-Only & No Short Selling**: Position size $\text{shares}_t \ge 0$ at all times.
2. **Zero Leverage**: Total market exposure $\le V_t$.
3. **Deterministic Integer Allocation**:
   When `Action 1 (BUY)` is triggered from cash:
   $$\text{max\_shares} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{cash}_t}{\text{Open}_{t+1} \cdot (1 + c)} \right\rfloor$$
   where $c = 0.001$ (0.10% transaction fee rate).
   - If $\text{max\_shares} \ge 1$: Execute purchase of $\Delta s = \text{max\_shares}$.
   - If $\text{max\_shares} < 1$: Cash is insufficient to purchase even 1 share; order is rejected as invalid, defaulting to `HOLD` with ₹0 fee.
4. **Churn Prevention (Unintended Repeated Trades)**:
   - If the agent selects `BUY` when it already holds shares (`self.shares > 0`), the environment rejects the trade, executes 0 transactions, and levies ₹0 cost.
   - If the agent selects `SELL` when it holds 0 shares (`self.shares == 0`), the trade is rejected, executes 0 transactions, and levies ₹0 cost.
   - This prevents artificial fee drainage caused by policy exploration or repeated signals.

## 4. Execution Timing Protocol

To prevent future lookahead leakage and realistically simulate trading operations, the environment enforces a dual-timestamp execution convention:

```
Day t Close                      Day t+1 Open                   Day t+1 Close
────────────                     ────────────                   ─────────────
• Indicators known up to t       • Order executed at Open(t+1)   • Portfolio marked at Close(t+1)
• State s_t constructed          • Shares updated               • V(t+1) = Cash + Shares * Close(t+1)
• Agent selects action a_t       • Fee (0.1%) deducted          • Reward r(t+1) = log(V(t+1) / V(t))
                                 • Overnight gap captured       • Next state s(t+1) constructed
```

1. **Information Horizon ($t$)**: Observation $s_t$ is constructed strictly from price and technical indicators available up to the close of day $t$. Neither $\text{Open}_{t+1}$ nor $\text{Close}_{t+1}$ is present in $s_t$.
2. **Execution Price ($t+1$ Open)**: Any order placed at day $t$ close is filled at the opening auction price $\text{Open}_{t+1}$. The overnight jump from $\text{Close}_t$ to $\text{Open}_{t+1}$ is correctly borne by held shares (or avoided by cash).
3. **Valuation & Reward ($t+1$ Close)**: The portfolio is marked to market at the end of the trading session using $\text{Close}_{t+1}$.

## 5. Reward Formulation

The environment implements the continuous logarithmic portfolio return as the step reward:
$$r_{t+1} = \log\left(\frac{V_{t+1}}{V_t}\right) = \log(V_{t+1}) - \log(V_t)$$
where:
$$V_{t} = \text{cash}_t + \text{shares}_t \cdot \text{Close}_t$$
$$V_{t+1} = \text{cash}_{t+1} + \text{shares}_{t+1} \cdot \text{Close}_{t+1}$$

### Mathematical Properties:
1. **Time Additivity**: The cumulative episodic reward over $T$ steps equals the exact total logarithmic return:
   $$\sum_{t=0}^{T-1} r_{t+1} = \log\left(\frac{V_T}{V_0}\right)$$
2. **Symmetric Penalty**: A $+10\%$ gain ($r = +0.0953$) followed by a $-10\%$ loss ($r = -0.1054$) yields a net negative reward ($r_{\text{total}} = -0.0101$), correctly penalizing downside volatility without requiring ad-hoc volatility penalties.
3. **Transaction Fee Penalization**: Since fees are deducted directly from cash at execution, $V_{t+1}$ drops by exactly the fee amount, penalizing excessive turnover naturally in the reward signal.
4. **Zero-Return Cash Stability**: When holding 100% cash, $V_{t+1} = V_t \implies r_{t+1} = \log(1.0) = 0.0$.

## 8. Known Limitations & Design Trade-offs

1. **Market Impact & Order Book Depth**: The environment assumes infinite liquidity at $\text{Open}_{t+1}$. For ₹100,000 capital in high-volume large-cap equities (e.g., RELIANCE, TCS), market impact is negligible; however, scaling to institutional sizes ($\ge ₹10\text{ crore}$) would require slippage modeling.
2. **Single-Asset Episode Scope**: Currently, each episode runs on a single equity stream. Portfolio diversification across multiple assets simultaneously is not modeled within a single step.
3. **Execution Latency**: Orders are executed at the Open auction. Intraday execution or stop-loss trigger events within the $(Open, High, Low, Close)$ bar are not simulated.
